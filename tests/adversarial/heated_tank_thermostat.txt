A process water heating skid consists of heated tank HT-5 with a floor area of 1.2 m2 and a height of 1.4 m, filled from a mains water supply through on/off fill valve FV-51, which passes 0.003 m3/s when open. An immersion heater EH-5 rated at 6 kW warms the water; thermostat TC-5 switches the heater on below 55 degC and off above 60 degC. Level transmitter LT-5 reports the tank level to controller PLC-5. When the operator presses START, PLC-5 opens FV-51 and keeps it open until the level reaches 0.9 m, then closes it and enables the heater. When TC-5 reports 60 degC, PLC-5 opens drain valve DV-52, which passes 0.005 m3/s when open, to discharge the hot water to the wash line until the level falls to 0.1 m. The tank starts empty at 0.05 m.
