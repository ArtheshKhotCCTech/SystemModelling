A rainwater buffer system collects roof runoff from a supply boundary through on/off inlet valve XV-301 into buffer tank BT-1, which has a cross-section of 2.5 m2 and a height of 1.5 m; level transmitter LT-301 reports the tank level to controller PLC-3, which opens XV-301 when the operator presses FILL, closes it when the level reaches 1.2 m, waits 30 s, and then opens outlet valve XV-302 to discharge the tank to a garden sink until the level falls to 0.2 m; XV-301 passes 0.004 m3/s and XV-302 passes 0.006 m3/s when open, and both valves are spring-return closed.
