Storage tank ST-7 has a cross-section of 3 and is filled from a supply through inlet valve IV-71, which passes 0.01 when open. Level transmitter LT-7 reports the tank level to controller PLC-7. PLC-7 opens IV-71 when the operator presses FILL and closes it when the level reaches 1.4. Outlet valve OV-72 drains ST-7 to a sink; PLC-7 opens OV-72 when the operator presses DRAIN and closes it when the tank is empty.
