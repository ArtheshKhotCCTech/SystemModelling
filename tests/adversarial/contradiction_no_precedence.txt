Collection tank CT-4 has a cross-section of 1.8 m2 and a height of 1.6 m and starts at a level of 0.1 m. It is filled from a supply header through on/off valve AV-41, which passes 0.004 m3/s when open, and emptied through on/off valve AV-42, which passes 0.005 m3/s when open, into a drain sink. Level transmitter LT-4 reports the level to controller PLC-4. When the operator presses FILL, PLC-4 opens AV-41. The high level setpoint of CT-4 is 1.0 m; at the high level PLC-4 closes AV-41 and opens AV-42 until the level falls to 0.2 m. For overflow protection the high level setpoint of CT-4 is 1.3 m.
